9. Cross-Role Relationships & End-to-End Workflows

The strength of the MCMS architecture lies in its rigorous cross-role handoffs. No single persona operates in isolation; academic integrity, quality verification, and credit mobility depend on multi-stage workflows spanning multiple actors, distinct pages, and transactional state changes.

9.1 End-to-End Multi-Stage Workflow Maps

Workflow 1: Curriculum Development, Review & Senate Approval

Participating Roles: Lecturer (Author), Peer Reviewer / Industry Expert (Evaluator), Deputy Dean (Endorser & Senate Recorder), Senate (Supreme Authority).

Workflow 2: Cohort Readiness, Enrolment & Delivery Handoff

Participating Roles: Lecturer (Coordinator), Deputy Dean (Verifier), Prospective Student (Learner).

Workflow 3: Assessment, Completion Confirmation & Credential Issuance

Participating Roles: Lecturer (Instructor), Deputy Dean (Academic Authority), Student (Graduate), Employer (Verifier).

Workflow 4: Academic Credit Banking (ACR), Consent & National ABC Deposit

Participating Roles: System (ACR Generator), Student (Owner & Consenter), Deputy Dean (Depositor), National ABC Bank.

Workflow 5: Credit Transfer Application, Equivalence & Degree Admission

Participating Roles: Student (Applicant), Deputy Dean of Receiving Faculty (Evaluator).

9.2 Disconnected Workflows & Continuity Gaps

The audit identified specific points where user workflows experience friction, incomplete automation, or disconnected handoffs:

  1. Quality Verification Re-Verification Task Handoff (FR-175): When an approved SAMC undergoes a material change, the system successfully flags the version as requiring re-verification and logs an event. However, it does not automatically instantiate a new draft QV record in qv_records; the lecturer must manually create a new portfolio from scratch.
  2. CQI Action to Change Request Bridge (EV-08): In the CQI module (MD-14), if an approved improvement action requires a curriculum alteration, the system creates a generic task reminder for the lecturer rather than directly pre-populating a draft Change Request in /my-mcs/{id}/change.
  3. Student Notice on Cohort Cancellation: When a Deputy Dean cancels an offering (POST /offerings/{id}/cancel), database records and payments are updated correctly, but the participant cancellation email notification relies on an inline job rather than an asynchronous event queue.
  4. Transfer Decision Letter PDF Generation (DOC-12): While the credit transfer decision is recorded and viewable in the web interface (/transfer/{id}), the formal Senate Transfer Decision Letter template remains unimplemented as a downloadable document.
