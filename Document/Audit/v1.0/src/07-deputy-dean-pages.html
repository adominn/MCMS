6. Deputy Dean Pages & Functionality Analysis

The Deputy Dean Academic (Persona P2) is the primary faculty academic authority in MCMS. The role oversees the entire lifecycle of micro-credentials within the faculty: curriculum endorsement, Senate decision recording, cohort readiness confirmation, assessment verification, completion confirmation, credential lifecycle governance, quality verification, ABC credit deposits, and credit transfer evaluations.

6.1 Proposal Endorsement & Senate Approval Recording (MD-03)

A. Page Information

  • Page Names: Approvals Hub (/approvals), Proposal Endorsement Review (/approvals/{id}), Assign Reviewers (POST /approvals/{id}/reviewers), Senate Recording (/approvals/{id}/senate), Decision Pack (/approvals/{id}/pack)
  • Role: Deputy Dean (deputy_dean)
  • Exact Routes: GET /approvals, GET /approvals/{id}, POST /approvals/{id}/reviewers, POST /approvals/{id}/endorse, POST /approvals/{id}/{how}, GET /approvals/{id}/senate, POST /approvals/{id}/senate, GET /approvals/{id}/pack
  • Page Type: Multi-Stage Approval Portal & Institutional Decision Recording
  • Related Database Tables: mc_versions, micro_credentials, review_assignments, reviews, review_comments, approval_decisions, tasks
  • Related Components & Controllers: App\Modules\Approval\ApprovalController, Views: approval.index, approval.show, approval.senate, approval.pack

B. Intended Functionality

Governs the faculty approval pipeline (FR-60 to FR-71): (1) Assign Reviewers: Selects one or more peer reviewers or industry experts with a target due date. (2) Endorsement Review: Inspects curriculum tabs, reviewer rubrics, and tracked comments. Verifies category classification (SAMC vs UMC). Resolves or enforces blocking comments. Actions: Endorse, Return for Amendment, or Do Not Support. (3) Senate Approval Recording: Senate is the supreme academic authority but does not log in (D-28). The Deputy Dean officially records the formal Senate decision (Meeting Reference, Senate Approval Date, Effective Date, Conditions). (4) Senate Decision Pack: Compiles an executive summary pack of the curriculum, outcomes, assessment matrix, and reviewer recommendations for submission to Senate.

C. Current Implementation

Fully functional and verified in Playwright flow FL-04 and scenario Act 1. Endorsement blocks if any reviewer comments marked Blocking remain unresolved. Self-endorsement is strictly prevented. Recording Senate approval transitions the version status to approved, making it eligible for cohort offerings.

D. Functionality Status

Status: Fully Working. Endorsement checks, comment resolution, Senate recording, and audit logging operate as specified.

B. Intended Functionality

Enforces the two-party institutional readiness check (FR-86). The Deputy Dean inspects the lecturer's readiness submission across 6 operational dimensions. If satisfied, the Deputy Dean records formal acceptance. Only when confirmed does the offering status transition to ready / open, automatically publishing it in the public catalogue. In emergencies, the Deputy Dean can cancel a cohort with a documented reason, automatically notifying participants and triggering refunds.

  • Role: Deputy Dean (deputy_dean)

  • Related Components & Controllers: ResultsController, CompletionController, Views: results.index, results.verify, completion.show

B. Intended Functionality

Core academic certification engine: (1) Results Verification: Inspects grade distributions, raw marks, and pass rates submitted by lecturers. Verifying marks generates official module_achievements and digital_badges. Learners under integrity holds are automatically bypassed. (2) Completion Confirmation: Verifies that the participant has achieved every required module, satisfied all whole-micro-credential assessments, and holds no open disciplinary cases. Formal confirmation sets enrolment status to completed, stores final weighted marks, and fires the CompletionConfirmed event (EV-31) — the sole automated trigger for digital credential issuance.

C. Current Implementation

Fully functional and verified in Playwright flow FL-08 and scenario Act 2. Results verification is atomic. The completion confirmation check dynamically validates all prerequisites before enabling the confirm button.

6.4 Credential Administration & Lifecycle Corrections (MD-09)

A. Page Information

  • Page Names: Credential Admin Directory (/admin/credentials), Credential Admin Detail (/admin/credentials/{id}), Correct (POST /admin/credentials/{id}/correct), Replace (POST /admin/credentials/{id}/replace), Revoke (POST /admin/credentials/{id}/revoke)
  • Role: Deputy Dean (deputy_dean)
  • Exact Routes: GET /admin/credentials, GET /admin/credentials/{id}, POST /admin/credentials/{id}/correct, POST /admin/credentials/{id}/replace, POST /admin/credentials/{id}/revoke
  • Page Type: Credential Governance & Cryptographic Status Operations
  • Related Database Tables: credentials, credential_revisions, enrolments, users
  • Related Components & Controllers: CredentialController@adminIndex, @adminShow, Views: credentials.admin, credentials.admin-show

B. Intended Functionality

Administers all issued micro-credentials within the faculty scope (FR-143 to FR-147). In the event of administrative errors, data updates, or academic misconduct, the Deputy Dean executes immutable lifecycle corrections: - Correction: Fixes typographical errors (e.g., student name spelling). Maintains identical verification URL; shows status Corrected with public revision audit history. - Replacement: Issues a new credential code for substantial structural changes. The old credential permanently indicates status Replaced pointing to the new reference. - Revocation: Immediately invalidates the credential. Public verification permanently shows status Revoked with documented legal justification. Nothing is ever hard-deleted.

C. Current Implementation

Fully functional and verified in Playwright flow FL-10 and scenario Act 2. Revisions are immutably logged in credential_revisions with user, reason, before/after JSON diffs, and timestamp.

D. Functionality Status

Status: Fully Working. Audit-trailed lifecycle operations operate reliably.

6.5 Academic Credit Record (ACR) & ABC Banking (MD-11)

A. Page Information

  • Page Names: Credit Bank Queue (/admin/creditbank), Confirm ACR (POST /admin/creditbank/acrs/{id}/confirm), Batch Export Detail (/admin/creditbank/batches/{id}), Record Deposit (POST /admin/creditbank/batches/{id}/deposit)
  • Role: Deputy Dean (deputy_dean)
  • Exact Routes: GET /admin/creditbank, POST /admin/creditbank/acrs/{id}/confirm, GET /admin/creditbank/batches/{id}, POST /admin/creditbank/batches/{id}/deposit
  • Page Type: National Credit Banking & Batch File Management
  • Related Database Tables: acrs, acr_checks, consents, abc_batches, abc_deposits, credit_ledger_entries
  • Related Components & Controllers: CreditBankController, Views: creditbank.queue, creditbank.batch

B. Intended Functionality

Oversees credit banking operations with the Malaysian Academic Credit Bank (ABC). The ACR is generated automatically following 7 automated checks (valid accreditation, credit-bearing category, no unresolved disciplinary cases). Once the student submits consent, the record enters the ABC Queue. The Deputy Dean confirms the record, exports compliant CSV batch files for ABC upload, and officially records the returned ABC deposit reference (e.g., ABC-FTMK-2026-03), activating the 10-year validity counter in the student's ledger.

C. Current Implementation

Fully functional and verified in Playwright flow FL-11 and scenario Act 3. Blocks any ACR where student consent is missing or declined. Deposit recording atomically creates credit ledger entries.

D. Functionality Status

Status: Fully Working. Export, confirmation, and ledger activation operate correctly.

6.6 Credit Transfer Evaluation & Programme Admission (MD-12)

A. Page Information

  • Page Names: Transfer Queue (/transfers), Evaluation Workspace (/transfers/{id}), Record Decision (POST /transfers/{id}/decide), Record Admission (POST /admissions)
  • Role: Deputy Dean (deputy_dean)
  • Exact Routes: GET /transfers, GET /transfers/{id}, POST /transfers/{id}/decide, POST /admissions
  • Page Type: Academic Credit Evaluation & Degree Admission Authority
  • Related Database Tables: transfer_applications, transfer_lines, equivalence_criteria, equivalence_assessments, transfer_decisions, admissions, credit_ledger_entries
  • Related Components & Controllers: EvaluationController, Views: transfer.queue, transfer.decide

B. Intended Functionality

Evaluates student applications to transfer micro-credential credit toward academic degree courses (FR-ct*). The Deputy Dean executes a rigorous evaluation: (1) Validity Check: Verifies ACR authenticity and validity period. (2) Equivalence Matrix: Evaluates 9 mandatory Appendix F7 criteria and compares micro-credential learning outcomes directly against target course syllabus outcomes. Approval is strictly blocked if any course learning outcome lacks a matching micro-credential outcome, regardless of credit hours. (3) Residency & Transfer Limits: Verifies that transferred credit does not breach the faculty 30% credit limit. (4) Decision: Approved, Partly Approved, More Evidence Needed, or Not Approved. Approval atomically updates the credit ledger to redeemed. (5) Admission Recording: Admission is a distinct fact that credit transfer never automatically creates (D-28). The Deputy Dean independently records the matriculation reference.

C. Current Implementation

Fully functional and verified in Playwright flow FL-12 and scenario Act 3. Outcome-based matching is strictly enforced by backend validation rules.

D. Functionality Status

Status: Fully Working. Rigorous evaluation logic and single redemption protection verified.
