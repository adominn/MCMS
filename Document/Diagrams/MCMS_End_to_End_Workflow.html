UTeM Micro-Credential Management System

Complete End-to-End Governance, Offering Setup, Grading, Digital Credentials & National ABC Credit Transfer Lifecycle across all institutional roles (Phases 1 – 7, 27 Steps).

PHASE 1
MC Proposal & Senate Approval
Category determination, 9-tab curriculum dossier, COI declaration, peer review appraisal, DD endorsement, and Senate minute pack.
7 Steps ▼
1
Lecturer Determine Category (UMC / SAMC / MCE)
Interactive wizard selects qualification pathway: Unbundled (UMC, credit-bearing linked to parent course), Standalone (SAMC, credit-bearing with Quality Verification), or Executive (MCE, non-credit).
🔗 /curriculum/wizard
2
Lecturer Fill 9-Tab Proposal Dossier
Complete all statutory tabs: General Info, Learning Outcomes, Market Need, Lampiran B Readiness (R1–R7), Assessment Strategy, Delivery, Articulation Pathways, Budget, and Evidence Uploads.
🔗 /curriculum/proposals/{id}
3
Deputy Dean Assign Reviewers & Evaluation Scope
Deputy Dean (Academic) assigns 2 internal or external subject matter experts; system triggers official evaluation invitations and statutory declarations.
🔗 /approval/proposals/{id}/reviewers
4
Reviewer Sign COI & Submit Evaluation Form
Expert reviewer signs statutory Conflict of Interest (COI) declaration, evaluates dossier against MQA micro-credential rubrics, and submits formal appraisal comments.
🔗 /reviews/{id}/coi 🔗 /reviews/{id}
5
Lecturer Respond to Reviewer Comments & Amend
Lecturer addresses appraisal recommendations, updates syllabus or assessment weightings, and enters formal line-by-line response remarks.
🔗 /curriculum/proposals/{id}
6
Deputy Dean Verify Feedback Resolution & Faculty Endorsement
Deputy Dean inspects reviewer resolution notes, verifies MQA compliance, and formally endorses the proposal forward to the University Senate.
🔗 /approval/proposals/{id}
7
Senate Record Senate Minutes & Issue Approval Pack
Senate Committee records official meeting minutes, Senate paper reference, and formally stamps curriculum approval, enabling the micro-credential for live cohort offerings.
🔗 /approval/proposals/{id}/senate
PHASE 2
Offering Setup & Readiness Sign-off
Cohort creation, Lampiran B readiness audit (R1–R7), and official catalogue publication.
3 Steps ▼
8
Lecturer Create Cohort Offering & Fill R1–R7 Checklist
Define cohort study dates, enrollment capacity, course fee, and link Lampiran B readiness evidence (Syllabus, LMS links, Staffing, QA).
🔗 /offerings/new 🔗 /offerings/{id}
9
Deputy Dean Inspect Evidence & Confirm Readiness
Deputy Dean audits all 7 readiness proofs (R1–R7) and signs statutory confirmation, transitioning status from Draft → Ready → Open.
🔗 /offerings/{id}
10
System / Admin Publish to Public & Learner Catalogue
Course is officially published to the public portal and internal catalogue with syllabus highlights, credit count, and tuition fees.
🔗 /public/catalogue 🔗 /catalogue
PHASE 3
Student Registration & Enrolment
Public catalogue discovery, PDPA statutory consent, gateway payment, and verified cohort enrolment.
4 Steps ▼
11
Student Browse Offering & Register (PDPA Consent)
Student browses offering, selects enrolment mode, provides identification details (IC/Passport), and signs statutory Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA 2010) consent.
🔗 /register/{offering}
12
Student Pay Course Fee via Gateway
Execute online payment via UTeM payment gateway (FPX / Credit Card) or submit institutional sponsorship guarantee letter.
🔗 /register/{offering} (Step 3: Pay)
13
Student / System Enrolment Confirmed & Confirmation Letter
System confirms registration reference, generates official Enrolment Confirmation PDF letter, issue receipt, and grants LMS access.
🔗 /enrolments/{id}/confirmation
14
Lecturer Access Cohort Roster & Monitor Attendance
Lecturer accesses live participant roster, verifies LMS onboarding, tracks coursework completion, and monitors learner engagement.
🔗 /offerings/{id}/participants
PHASE 4
Grading & Results Approval (Gate 1)
Modular mark entry, assessment weights, academic integrity resolution, and statutory mark verification.
3 Steps · Gate 1 ▼
15
Lecturer Key in Modular Marks & Whole-MC Scores
Lecturer grades each student across modular assessment components (e.g. 4 continuous labs) and the whole-MC Capstone; system computes weighted score, letter grade, and grade points (4.00 scale).
🔗 /offerings/{id}/results
16
Deputy Dean GATE 1 Verify Marks & Resolve Integrity Cases
Statutory Gate 1: Deputy Dean audits score distribution, resolves plagiarism or academic integrity flags, endorses results, and mints module-level digital badges.
🔗 /results 🔗 /results/{id}
17
Student View Published Verified Grades
Student logs in to view official breakdown of verified marks, letter grade (e.g. A / 4.00), examiner comments, and earned module badges.
🔗 /my-results 🔗 /my-results/{id}
PHASE 5
Cohort Completion & Digital Credentials (Gate 2)
Statutory completion confirmation, Open Badges 3.0 auto-minting, A4 certificate, and VC-JWT public verification.
3 Steps · Gate 2 ▼
18
Deputy Dean GATE 2 Confirm Completion & Authorize Minting
Statutory Gate 2: Deputy Dean verifies whole-MC requirements (passing all components + Capstone), authorizes graduation, and signs cryptographic credential issuance.
🔗 /completion/{id}
19
Student Access Open Badges 3.0 & A4 Certificate
Student downloads the official bilingual A4 Certificate PDF and accesses 1EdTech Open Badges 3.0 with complete metadata (criteria, assertion, issuer, recipient hash).
🔗 /credentials 🔗 /credentials/{id}
20
Public / Verifier Verify Cryptographic VC-JWT Signatures
External employers and universities verify certificate and badge authenticity without an account using UTeM's public JWKS endpoint (`/.well-known/jwks.json`).
🔗 /verify
PHASE 6
Academic Credit Record (ACR) & National ABC Deposit
ACR generation, student deposit consent, and ABC batch export with 10-year credit storage.
3 Steps ▼
21
Student View ACR & Submit Consent to Deposit
Student reviews the Academic Credit Record (MQF level, credit value, 120 SLT hours, assessment breakdown) and chooses statutory consent: "I consent", "Postpone", or "Decline".
🔗 /my-credit 🔗 /my-credit/{id}
22
Deputy Dean Confirm Queue & Export ABC CSV Batch
Deputy Dean audits consent queue, records National ABC deposit reference (e.g. ABC-2026-UTEM-0042), or downloads MQA batch CSV format.
🔗 /deposits 🔗 /deposits/{id}
23
Student Credits Added to Ledger (10-Yr Validity)
Academic credit is flagged as Available on the student's credit ledger with a 10-year statutory validity countdown ready for university degree articulation.
🔗 /my-credit
PHASE 7
Credit Transfer & Degree Exemption (Gate 3)
Articulation matching (≥80% learning outcome match), single redemption lock, and official DOC-12 decision letter.
4 Steps · Gate 3 ▼
24
Student Apply for Transfer to Degree Programme
Student selects target degree (e.g. BITS Software Development), ticks available deposited micro-credentials, selects target degree courses to exempt, and provides syllabus mapping evidence.
🔗 /transfer 🔗 /transfer/new
25
Deputy Dean GATE 3 Evaluate 80% LO Match & Single Redemption
Statutory Gate 3: Receiving faculty evaluates ≥80% learning outcome equivalency match; system enforces single redemption lock so credits can never be used twice.
🔗 /transfers 🔗 /transfers/{id}
26
Deputy Dean Issue Official Decision Letter (DOC-12)
System formats and issues the official bilingual Credit Transfer Decision Letter (DOC-12) detailing approved course exemptions, credit waivers, and faculty conditions.
🔗 /transfers/{id}/letter
27
Student View Approved Course Exemptions
Student views confirmed credit exemption; status transitions from Available → Provisionally redeemed → Redeemed upon official programme matriculation.
🔗 /transfer/{id}
