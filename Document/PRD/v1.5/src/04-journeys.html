4. User Journeys

Journeys show the experience of a persona across several features. The target experience rates each step with MCMS on a scale of 1 (frustrating) to 5 (effortless). Steps that stay at 3 are limited by processes outside MCMS, such as committee meetings, which MCMS records but does not control. Each journey crosses several recognition stages; the stage column shows that the participant or officer always knows which stage they are in.

4.4 Employer: Verifying a Credential

Tan opens the link or scans the QR code on a certificate. The verification page loads immediately without login and presents an authoritative, centered certificate-style result slip. It features a single prominent status seal (e.g. Status: Valid in emerald), a copyable Crockford Base32 verification code, and a clean two-column summary of the micro-credential, holder, issuer, issue date, and graduating credits. The interface removes distracting implementation pills (such as Ed25519 or RFC specifications) and provides direct action buttons to download the verifiable cryptographic files (.json and .jwt) for HR archival, print the official slip, or review the statutory Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA) notice ({{fr-vffields}}). If the credential was revoked or replaced, the status seal clearly states so with the effective date. The whole verification journey takes under a minute, needs no contact with UTeM, and resolves cleanly across desktop and mobile devices.
