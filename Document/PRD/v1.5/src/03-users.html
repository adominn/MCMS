3. Users and Personas

3.1 Users and Stakeholder Map

MCMS strictly adheres to the official role classifications defined in Garis Panduan Micro-Credential UTeM Versi 1.0 Appendix H (D-28, D-54, D-60), formalizing eight distinct roles across the academic governance hierarchy: Participant / Student (P8), Course Coordinator / Lecturer (P1), Faculty Management / Deputy Dean (P2), Senate Academic Representative / Academic Authority (P3), Quality Assurance Authority / QA Officer (P4), Platform & Technical Support / CODL Admin (P5), Records & Credential Custodian (P6), and Authorized ABC Depositor (P7). External engagement is supported via Industry Expert / SME (X2, comments only) and unauthenticated Employers / Public Verifiers (X3). The legacy separate receiving programme evaluator role (X4) is officially deprecated, with credit transfer equivalence evaluated by CODL Admin (P5) and approved by Senate (P3), while receiving faculty Deputy Deans (P2) monitor transfer articulation volume through a dedicated read-only Faculty Programme Overview workspace (D-61). The system generates credentials and ACR automatically after P1 Course Coordinator sign-off and P2 Deputy Dean completion confirmation (D-29, D-55), while P6/P7 confirms ACRs and records ABC deposits independently of faculty academic evaluation (D-54). {{fig:stakeholders}} places the users by how often they use MCMS and how much their work depends on it.
