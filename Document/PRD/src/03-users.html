3. Users and Personas

3.1 Users and Stakeholder Map

MCMS has six kinds of user (SRS Section 2.5, decision D-28): students, lecturers, deputy deans of the PTj, the Senate (which approves but does not log in), CODL as system administrator and technical operator only, and employers or the public who verify credentials. The system itself issues credentials and generates ACR automatically after the deputy dean verifies (D-29). {{fig:stakeholders}} places the users by how often they use MCMS and how much their work depends on it.
