6. User Stories

Each story states a need in the user's words and gives acceptance criteria in Given / When / Then form. The SRS column lists the requirements the story delivers; a story is accepted only when those requirements are also met. All stories are Release 1 unless marked (R2). Story IDs are stable once issued.
