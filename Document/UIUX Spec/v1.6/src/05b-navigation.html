5. Navigation

Each role has its own collapsible side navigation (toggled via the top-left #nav-toggle control), grouped by work area, with its role-appropriate home page linked from both the brand header and navigation (D-46, D-47). Live task counts appear beside deputy dean and lecturer action items and fall as work completes. The global search bar scopes strictly inside the signed-in role: CODL administrators search users, settings, uploaded UCreds files, micro-credentials and cohorts; academic roles search micro-credentials, learners, cohorts and credentials within scope.

Side navigation by role

Discrepancy logging shares the unified Traceability and records workspace (/records/trace) via horizontal tabs rather than duplicating a sidebar item (D-47). Task queues for non-academic roles hide zero-count academic area tabs and render plain-language area labels such as Quality Verification (D-46).
