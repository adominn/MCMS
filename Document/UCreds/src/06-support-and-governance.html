6. Student Support Infrastructure, Knowledge Base & Policies

6.1 Central Helpdesk & Technical Support

To ensure high learner retention and immediate issue resolution across global time zones, CODL operates a multi-tiered support helpdesk directly linked to the UCreds navigation bar:

  • Support Email: ucreds.helpdesk@utem.edu.my (Standard SLA: Response within 24 operational hours; error reports require mandatory screenshot attachments).
  • Direct Telephone Hotline: 06-229 2272.
  • Operational Hours: 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM, Monday through Friday (Malaysia Time, UTC/GMT +8:00).
  • Virtual Companion / Chatbot: Embedded 24/7 interactive automated guide assisting learners with navigation, login troubleshooting, and course enrolment guidance.

6.3 Key Institutional Policies and Regulations

Audit of the published articles reveals strict adherence to UTeM Senate postgraduate academic regulations:

  1. Academic Regulations & Grading: ODL postgraduate courses adhere to the standard UTeM grading scheme where Grade B (CGPA 3.00) is the minimum passing grade for credit accumulation toward degree conferment.
  2. Virtual Library Access: Learners gain authenticated access to UTeM's digital library portal (https://library.utem.edu.my/), providing IEEE Xplore, ScienceDirect, and Scopus subscriptions via institutional EZproxy and VPN tunnels.
  3. Campus VPN Security: Off-campus distance learners are provisioned with secure, encrypted tunnel access to UTeM's internal intranet devices, protecting against man-in-the-middle attacks and session eavesdropping.
  4. Official ODL Handbook: The complete operational handbook is accessible at: https://codl.utem.edu.my/02/wp-content/uploads/OPEN-AND-DISTANCE-LEARNING-HANDBOOK-V1-2025.pdf.
