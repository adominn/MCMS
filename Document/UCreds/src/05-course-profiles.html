ParameterSpecification / Details
Programme CodeMTDL (Open and Distance Learning)
Accreditation Ref.MQA/PA17473 (Malaysian Qualifications Agency Registered)
Total Tuition FeeMYR 25,110.00 (Local Learner Schedule)
Host FacultyFaculty of Information and Communication Technology (FTMK)

Target AudienceData Analysts, IT Executives, Statisticians, and Software Engineers seeking advanced analytics expertise.

5.3 Profile 3: First Order Differential Equations [ID 155] (Mathematics Series)

ParameterSpecification / Details
Course LevelIntermediate | Micro-Course
Student Learning Time18 Hours 00 Minutes
Enrolment FeeMYR 12.00
InstructorsKhoo Chin Foon, Loh Ser Lee, Irma Wani Binti Jamaludin, Rahifa Binti Ranom, Suhaila Binti Salleh (Centre for Mathematical Sciences)
PrerequisitesBasic Single-Variable Differential and Integral Calculus.
Syllabus BreakdownPart 1: Introduction and classification of ordinary differential equations (order and linearity). Part 2: Exact differential equations and integrating factors. Part 3: Separable equations, homogeneous substitutions, and linear first-order differential equations with real-world engineering boundary conditions.
Micro Learning Outcomes (MLOs)
  • Classify differential equations according to order, degree, and linearity.
  • Determine whether a given first-order differential equation is exact and apply integrating factors when inexact.
  • Solve initial value problems (IVPs) modeling physical electrical circuits (RL/RC) and thermal cooling systems.

ParameterSpecification / Details
Course LevelIntermediate / Modular Postgraduate Micro-Credential
Student Learning Time15 Hours SLT
Enrolment FeeMYR 12.00
Lead InstructorDr. Noor Fazilla Abd Yusof (Faculty of Information and Communication Technology)
PrerequisitesFoundations of Python Programming and Linear Algebra.

ParameterSpecification / Details
