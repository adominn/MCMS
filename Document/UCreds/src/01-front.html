1. Executive Summary & Audit Scope

1.1 Executive Summary

The Centre for Open and Distance Learning (CODL) at Universiti Teknikal Malaysia Melaka (UTeM) operates UCreds (https://ucreds.utem.edu.my/), the university's flagship digital learning and micro-credential delivery platform. Designed to advance flexible, lifelong, and industry-relevant education, UCreds bridges the gap between modular upskilling and fully accredited academic awards.

This audit delivers an exhaustive, multi-dimensional assessment of the entire public reach of UCreds. Over the course of the investigation, 100% of the public catalog was explored, resulting in a verified registry of 60 curated public offerings (spanning 3 fully accredited ODL Master degree programmes, 2 structured learning paths, and 55 specialised micro-courses) alongside an underlying Moodle repository comprising 214 academic modules. In addition, the student support knowledge base (hosting 36 comprehensive policy and guidance articles) was thoroughly catalogued and synthesized.

1.2 Audit Objectives

The primary objectives of this technical and academic audit are as follows:

  • Exhaustive Inventory: Identify, catalog, and classify every course, micro-credential, learning path, and academic degree offered on the public UCreds portal, including prices, prerequisites, durations, and learning outcomes.
  • Platform & Infrastructure Analysis: Document the underlying software architecture, Learning Management System (LMS) engine, theme customisations, identity federation (Single Sign-On), and network security perimeter.
  • Pedagogical & Regulatory Alignment: Evaluate how UCreds implements the Malaysian Qualifications Agency (MQA) micro-credential framework, the Single Redemption Principle, and the Stackable Degree Pathway as mandated by the Garis Panduan Micro-Credential UTeM.
  • Support & Governance Review: Map the student onboarding ecosystem, eCounseling services, academic regulations, digital library facilities, and helpdesk operations.
  • MCMS Integration Blueprint: Formulate concrete integration specifications to bind UCreds seamlessly with the upcoming Micro-Credential Management System (MCMS) across course offering synchronization, batch grade transfers, and Academic Credit Record (ACR) deposits.

1.3 Scope and Methodology

The investigation utilized an automated, high-precision inspection pipeline combining headless browser automation (Playwright Core over Microsoft Edge), REST API introspection, DOM structure parsing, and network traffic interception. All data was cross-referenced across three distinct architectural layers:

  1. The Public Dynamic Catalog: Interrogated via the asynchronous catalog endpoint (/catalog/action.php) across all category filters and pagination matrices.
  2. The Course Overview Subsystem: Crawled across all individual course profile endpoints (/courses/overview.php?id={id}) to extract syllabi, instructors, and learning outcomes.
  3. The Integrated Knowledge Base: Queried via the WordPress REST API (/support/student/wp-json/wp/v2/docs) to extract all orientation handbooks, mental wellness procedures, and IT governance rules.
