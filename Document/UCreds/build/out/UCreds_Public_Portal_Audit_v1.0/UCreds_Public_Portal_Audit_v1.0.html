1. Executive Summary & Audit Scope

1.1 Executive Summary

The Centre for Open and Distance Learning (CODL) at Universiti Teknikal Malaysia Melaka (UTeM) operates UCreds (https://ucreds.utem.edu.my/), the university's flagship digital learning and micro-credential delivery platform. Designed to advance flexible, lifelong, and industry-relevant education, UCreds bridges the gap between modular upskilling and fully accredited academic awards.

This audit delivers an exhaustive, multi-dimensional assessment of the entire public reach of UCreds. Over the course of the investigation, 100% of the public catalog was explored, resulting in a verified registry of 60 curated public offerings (spanning 3 fully accredited ODL Master degree programmes, 2 structured learning paths, and 55 specialised micro-courses) alongside an underlying Moodle repository comprising 214 academic modules. In addition, the student support knowledge base (hosting 36 comprehensive policy and guidance articles) was thoroughly catalogued and synthesized.

1.2 Audit Objectives

The primary objectives of this technical and academic audit are as follows:

  • Exhaustive Inventory: Identify, catalog, and classify every course, micro-credential, learning path, and academic degree offered on the public UCreds portal, including prices, prerequisites, durations, and learning outcomes.
  • Platform & Infrastructure Analysis: Document the underlying software architecture, Learning Management System (LMS) engine, theme customisations, identity federation (Single Sign-On), and network security perimeter.
  • Pedagogical & Regulatory Alignment: Evaluate how UCreds implements the Malaysian Qualifications Agency (MQA) micro-credential framework, the Single Redemption Principle, and the Stackable Degree Pathway as mandated by the Garis Panduan Micro-Credential UTeM.
  • Support & Governance Review: Map the student onboarding ecosystem, eCounseling services, academic regulations, digital library facilities, and helpdesk operations.
  • MCMS Integration Blueprint: Formulate concrete integration specifications to bind UCreds seamlessly with the upcoming Micro-Credential Management System (MCMS) across course offering synchronization, batch grade transfers, and Academic Credit Record (ACR) deposits.

1.3 Scope and Methodology

The investigation utilized an automated, high-precision inspection pipeline combining headless browser automation (Playwright Core over Microsoft Edge), REST API introspection, DOM structure parsing, and network traffic interception. All data was cross-referenced across three distinct architectural layers:

  1. The Public Dynamic Catalog: Interrogated via the asynchronous catalog endpoint (/catalog/action.php) across all category filters and pagination matrices.
  2. The Course Overview Subsystem: Crawled across all individual course profile endpoints (/courses/overview.php?id={id}) to extract syllabi, instructors, and learning outcomes.
  3. The Integrated Knowledge Base: Queried via the WordPress REST API (/support/student/wp-json/wp/v2/docs) to extract all orientation handbooks, mental wellness procedures, and IT governance rules.

2. System Architecture & Technical Infrastructure

2.1 Platform Overview & Technology Stack

UCreds is deployed as a dual-tier web architecture hosted on UTeM's institutional enterprise server infrastructure. The platform pairs a customized learning management engine with an integrated content management knowledge base to deliver both pedagogical content and administrative student guidance.

Core Technology Stack of the UCreds Platform

2.2 Identity Federation and Single Sign-On (SSO)

Authentication on UCreds is bifurcated to accommodate both internal university stakeholders and external lifelong learners:

  • Institutional Users (Staff & Students): Authenticate via the OpenID Connect (OIDC) protocol through Microsoft Entra ID (formerly Azure Active Directory) at endpoint /auth/oidc/. Upon successful federated authentication, accounts are automatically provisioned with university role mappings and linked to UTeM email domains.
  • Lifelong / Industry Learners: Authenticate via Google OAuth2 federation (/auth/oauth2/) or standard internal Moodle credentials (/login/index.php), allowing public candidates to register and purchase modular courses without requiring university matriculation.

2.3 Data Flow & Catalog Retrieval Model

Unlike standard Moodle installations where courses are viewed strictly through a static category hierarchy (/course/index.php), UCreds decouples the public exploration layer from the underlying LMS course structure. When a prospective learner visits https://ucreds.utem.edu.my/catalog/, the client executes an asynchronous POST request to action.php.

2.4 Security Perimeter & Network Isolation

The UCreds platform is guarded by an institutional Web Application Firewall (WAF). During the initial phases of this audit, standard headless automation tools (such as unmodified Puppeteer or Playwright instances) were intercepted with HTTP 500 error responses bearing signature Attack ID: 20000051. The perimeter enforces stringent checks on:

  1. User-Agent Integrity: Requires verified modern browser signatures (e.g., Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge desktop strings).
  2. TLS Fingerprinting: Inspects JA3/TLS handshake ciphers to filter out unauthorized automated scrapers.
  3. Rate-Limiting: Restricts burst requests across catalog search endpoints to protect backend PHP-FPM workers.

3. Academic Framework, Stackable Pathways & Credit Governance

3.1 Micro-Credential Architecture at UTeM

In accordance with the Garis Panduan Micro-Credential Universiti Teknikal Malaysia Melaka and the Malaysian Qualifications Agency (MQA) Guidelines to Good Practices: Micro-credentials, UTeM classifies micro-credentials into distinct academic tiers designed to serve both professional upskilling and accredited degree attainment.

Core Pedagogical Principle: A micro-credential at UTeM is defined as a digital credential certifying the achievement of coherent, verifiable learning outcomes equivalent to a defined volume of student learning time (SLT). One academic credit corresponds strictly to 40 hours of SLT.

3.2 The Stackable Degree Pathway

A central pillar of UCreds is the Stackable Degree Pathway (Laluan Ijazah Bertingkat). Rather than requiring prospective students to commit to a multi-year master's programme from day one, learners can acquire standalone micro-credentials at their own pace. When assembled according to an approved programme structure, these credits can be transferred toward a full degree.

3.3 The Journey of a Credit in UCreds

A student or lifelong learner who completes a course on UCreds navigates a defined credit lifecycle governed by academic quality assurance rules:

  1. Enrolment & Assessment: The learner registers for the course (either free of charge or upon paying the course fee, e.g. RM 12.00 to RM 150.00). Assessment tasks (quizzes, practical lab assignments, capstone projects) are graded by course facilitators.
  2. Digital Badging & Internal Achievement Record: Upon achieving the passing threshold (minimum Grade C / 50%), UCreds issues a Digital Badge and records the module completion in the learner's Rekod Pencapaian Pembelajaran (Internal Achievement Record).
  3. The Choice: Kept on Record vs. Deposited to Academic Bank of Credit (ABC):
    • Kept on Record (Penyimpanan Dalaman): The learner retains their digital badge and certificate for career upskilling, resume enhancement, or professional development. No formal academic credit transfer is initiated. This carries zero administrative deposit fees and has indefinite validity within UTeM's continuing education registry.
    • Deposited to Academic Bank of Credit (Deposit ABC): If the completed module is quality-verified and accredited (UMC or SAMC), an official Academic Credit Record (ACR) is prepared. Once deposited into the national Academic Bank of Credit, the credits become available for formal credit transfer (Pindahan Kredit) toward a degree at UTeM or any participating higher education institution in Malaysia.
  4. The Single Redemption Principle: In compliance with MQA governance [Guide Clause 12.9], an academic credit can only be counted once toward the award of a formal academic qualification. Once a credit is redeemed toward an awarded degree, its status transitions permanently to Redeemed and it cannot be re-used for a second qualification.

3.4 Accreditation Status & Quality Verification

Offerings on UCreds are rigorously bench-marked against national regulatory standards:

  • MQA Provisional & Full Accreditation: Flagship postgraduate degrees offered via Open and Distance Learning carry formal accreditation. For instance, the Master of Technology in Data Science and Analytics bears accreditation reference number MQA/PA17473.
  • APEL Integration (Prior Experiential Learning): In accordance with national lifelong learning mandates, admission into UCreds ODL programmes supports APEL.A (Accreditation of Prior Experiential Learning for Access) for candidates lacking formal academic prerequisites, as well as APEL.C (Credit Award) allowing work experience to substitute for specific modular requirements.

8. Strategic Alignment & Integration with UTeM MCMS

8.1 The Architectural Dichotomy: UCreds vs. MCMS

A critical outcome of this audit is clarifying the distinct institutional roles played by UCreds and the upcoming Micro-Credential Management System (MCMS):

  • UCreds (The Learning Platform Gateway): UCreds is the customer-facing Learning Management System (LMS) and storefront. Its responsibility is content delivery, learner interaction, automated quiz evaluation, forum discussions, and digital badging.
  • MCMS (The Authoritative Credential Registry): MCMS is the central academic governance engine. It manages curriculum approval workflows (Faculty Deputy Dean & Senate), enforces the Single Redemption Principle, verifies quality standards (UMC vs SAMC), orchestrates graduation credit transfer, and deposits certified Academic Credit Records (ACR) to the national Academic Bank of Credit (ABC).

8.2 Data Entity Mapping

To ensure seamless interoperability between UCreds and MCMS, UCreds data objects map directly into the core entities specified in the MCMS Software Requirements Specification (SRS v1.0) and Software Design Document (SDD v1.0):

8.3 Automated Synchronization Architecture

MCMS incorporates scheduled background jobs designed specifically to ingest and reconcile learner outcomes from UCreds:

  1. Job JB-02 (LMS Grade Sync): Periodically polls the UCreds completion API or processes automated CSV gradebook exports to update student progression status in MCMS.
  2. Job JB-03 (ABC Deposit Dispatcher): Identifies eligible completed credentials that students have opted to deposit into the Academic Bank of Credit, signs the Academic Credit Record (ACR) with institutional keys, and transmits the payload to the national depository.

8.4 Conclusion and Strategic Recommendations

This comprehensive audit affirms that UCreds represents a highly mature, academically robust, and technically well-architected digital learning ecosystem. To maximize its impact and accelerate integration with MCMS, the following strategic actions are recommended for the Centre for Open and Distance Learning (CODL):

  1. Formalize REST API Endpoints: Transition the current batch CSV grade export to standard RESTful webhooks in Moodle (utilizing core web services with token authentication), allowing MCMS to ingest completion events in near real-time.
  2. Unified Taxonomy Standard: Standardize course naming across Moodle so that every micro-course metadata record explicitly references its parent subject module code (e.g., [MAXL5123]) and SLT hours.
  3. Automated APEL & Credit Transfer Portals: Surface a direct link on each UCreds course overview page enabling learners to preview how their earned micro-credential stacks into UTeM's degree programmes via the upcoming MCMS public portal.
