3.7 Assessment and Achievement Verification (M8)

Recognition follows achievement, not attendance [G 6.1, 8.6]. Results are entered or imported, then verified by the owning PTj before any badge, certificate or Digital Credential is issued. Verification links the participant's identity, the assessment evidence and the result [G 10.4].

3.8 Achievement Record (M9)

The Achievement Record (Rekod Pencapaian Pembelajaran Micro-Credential UTeM) is UTeM's internal record of modules completed, results, badges and MC completion. It is not an ACR and it is not evidence that credit was deposited to ABC or accepted by a programme [G 8.7.5, 18.3]. {{fig:umcflow}} shows how a modular UMC builds up to completion.

3.9 Credential Issuance and Verification (M10)

3.10 Quality Verification Management for SAMC (M11)

Quality Verification (QV) is the quality check for a SAMC carried out by the QVC under MQA rules. It is separate from UTeM's approval to offer: approval lets a micro-credential be offered; QV confirms its quality and gives it a credit value and MQF level [G 11.2]. QV requires at least one completed cohort and is valid for at most three continuous years [G 11.2.1–11.2.2]. In Release 1, MCMS prepares and tracks; UTeM staff submit to QVC/MQA outside the system (D-14).
