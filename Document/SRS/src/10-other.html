8.3 Acceptance Approach

A separate Test Plan is not part of the controlling document set; acceptance tests are traced directly from the requirement IDs of this SRS (D-01). For Release 1, acceptance requires that:

  1. every SHALL requirement of Sections 3, 5, 6 and 7 has at least one passing acceptance test that names its ID;
  2. every business rule of Section 4 has at least one passing automated test, including a test that the system refuses the forbidden case (for example, an ACR for an MCE, or reuse of redeemed credit);
  3. an end-to-end walkthrough of {{fig:journey}} is completed in the test environment with stub adapters;
  4. open items that block a Release 1 requirement (Appendix C) are either resolved or covered by the documented working assumption.
