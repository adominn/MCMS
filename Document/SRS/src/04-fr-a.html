3. System Features and Functional Requirements

This section details the functional requirements of MCMS, organised by system feature in the order of the micro-credential life cycle. Each subsection begins with a short description of the feature and, where it helps, a diagram, followed by a requirement table. Each requirement has a unique ID (FR-XX) for traceability. The business rules that these requirements enforce are listed with their own IDs in Section 4.

3.1 Identity, Roles and Access (M1)

Staff and participants sign in with their Microsoft education account through UTeM single sign-on; MCMS keeps no passwords of its own in production (D-10). Access is controlled by role and by scope: a person sees and acts only on the PTj and micro-credentials assigned to them. Approving bodies and role holders are settings, so a change in the University's committees does not need a code change (D-11, D-12).

3.2 Category Determination and Proposal (M2, M3)

A micro-credential starts as a proposal prepared by a lecturer in a guided workspace that follows Guide Appendix A. The first step is to determine the category, because the category decides the design rules, the approval route, the quality route and the possible recognition [G 6.5]. 3.2.3 UMC Unbundling and Module Structure

A UMC must keep a clear academic link with its parent course: learning outcome mapping, SLT, content and assessment [G 6.3.1]. The unbundling model decides how modules and assessment are arranged [G 6.3.1.1].

3.3 Review, Approval and Versioning (M4)

Every micro-credential must be reviewed and approved by the authority set by UTeM before it is offered [G 9.4–9.5]. Approval applies to one exact version of the structure. A material change creates a new version that needs review and re-approval before it takes effect [G 17.7–17.8]. {{fig:propstate}} shows the life of a proposal version.

3.4 Offering, Catalogue and Readiness (M5)

An approved version is offered as one or more cohorts. Before registration opens, the PTj confirms that materials, platform, assessment, facilitators, registration, records and certification are ready, using the checklist of Guide Appendix B [G 9.6]. Participants must receive honest, complete information before they enrol [G 10.3.1].

3.5 Registration, Enrolment and Cohorts (M6)

Participants register for open offerings after reading the offering information. Fees are handled through the payment adapter; in Release 1 a dummy payment service records simulated payments (A-03). For a modular UMC, participants may complete modules in different periods, and each module status is kept separately [G 10.3, 12.2].

3.6 Learning Platform Connection (M7)

Learning takes place on UCreds, the pilot learning platform. MCMS exchanges enrolments, progress, module completion, results and badge data with UCreds through the learning platform adapter. Because the connection method is not yet known, Release 1 uses CSV files (D-13, A-04, OI-04). Imported data is never final: it lands as provisional data awaiting verification (Section 3.7).
