1. Introduction

1.1 Purpose

This document fixes the vocabulary and the key decisions that the three controlling documents of the UTeM Micro-Credential Management System (MCMS), namely the Software Requirements Specification (SRS), the Product Requirements Document (PRD) and the Software Design Description (SDD), shall follow. Every later document shall use the terms in the Glossary with exactly these meanings and shall not contradict a decision in Section 3 unless the decision is first changed here and the version of this document is raised.

The document set is written for two readers at once: a supervisor who reviews it, and AI development agents and developers who use it as the guide, reference and rule set for building the system. For that reason requirements are written as single, identified statements with traceability to the Guide.

Appendix A: UCreds Content

This appendix records what the UCreds public portal contains and how it works, as found by the UCreds Public Portal Audit v1.0 of 27 October 2026 (D-34). The audit used only public pages. Where this appendix and the live portal differ, the live portal is right, and CODL's catalogue replaces this appendix when it is supplied.

A.1 The platform

A.2 What the catalogue contains

The audit states 60 public offerings: 3 ODL Master programmes, 2 structured learning paths and 55 specialised micro-courses, over an underlying Moodle repository of 214 course instances. Fees range from free to RM 150.00 for micro-courses, and up to RM 25,110.00 for a full programme.

A.3 Programmes, subjects and subject codes

Three programmes are delivered by Open and Distance Learning. The audit profiles two of them. A subject is a course of 120 hours, which is 3 credits at 40 hours a credit (D-27). Subject codes begin with the programme prefix (MAXL for the data science programme, MPSW for the shared research course).

The audit shows the stackable route of the data science programme in four tiers: standalone micro-courses of 10 to 20 hours (for example MAXL5123 Big Data Management Demystified, Document and Graph Databases, Hadoop Core Technologies, Spark Data Analysis), a subject of 120 hours and 3 credits (MAXL5123 Big Data Management), the core curriculum of 40 to 42 credits, and the accredited award. In MCMS this is the pathway of MD-13: a mapping transfers no credit, and each credit still goes through credit transfer evaluation (MD-12).

A.4 The public course directory

The tables below are the directory of the audit, without the author column. Each row has the Moodle course ID, the title (a subject code may be part of the title), the level, the student learning time and the fee.
