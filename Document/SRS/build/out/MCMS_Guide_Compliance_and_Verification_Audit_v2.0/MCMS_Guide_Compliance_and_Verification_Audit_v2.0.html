1.5 Key Findings & Structural Insights

  • Complete Alignment with Core Academic Rules: The system rigorously enforces the guide's foundational mandates. UMC parent course linking, 40-hour SLT thresholds, strict MCE non-credit separation, QV-gated credit granting for SAMC, automated ACR generation, and single redemption tracking are completely locked down in code and database constraints.
  • Separation of Academic Duties (Decision D-12 & Bab 15.4): The system strictly obeys the rule that CODL has zero academic approval authority. Curriculum proposals, offerings readiness, result verification, and credit transfers can only be endorsed, confirmed, and approved by Faculty Deputy Deans and Senate.
  • The Four True Software Gaps (FAIL):
    1. Direct National ABC API Adapter (Bab 12.5, 18.6): The system currently exports a CSV batch for manual submission to ABC because the MQA national ABC API specification is not yet published. The queue, export, and deposit confirmation exist, but not live machine-to-machine sync.
    2. Third-Party External Institution Credit Intake (Bab 12.7, 13.2, Rajah 18): Credit transfer currently accepts only internal UTeM ACRs. Ingesting transcripts from external universities is architecturally scheduled for Release 2.
    3. Automated Physical Evidence Upload & Virus Scanning (Lampiran D, B3, B4): Evidences in QV and offerings are currently maintained as authenticated metadata references, URLs, and notes rather than multipart file binaries with virus scanning.
    4. 1-Click Digital Wallet Export (Bab 8.7.1, Lampiran E1): Open Badges 3.0 / W3C VC JSON-LD and VC-JWT sheets can be downloaded and verified via QR, but native push to Apple Wallet / Google Wallet is not yet integrated.
  • Additional Capabilities Built in MCMS Beyond the Guide: MCMS includes critical capabilities not mentioned in the guide, such as the Demo Clock (advancing time to test 10-year expirations), SHA-256 Hash-Chained Audit Logs, Automated Record Mismatch Reconciliation, and Bi-directional UCreds LMS Course Synchronization.

2. Guide Structure, Architectural Models & Diagram Analysis

2.1 Structure of Garis Panduan Micro-Credential UTeM

The reference document, Garis Panduan Micro-Credential UTeM (Versi 1.0, 18 September 2026), published by the Centre for Open and Distance Learning (CODL), establishes the definitive institutional framework for micro-credentials at Universiti Teknikal Malaysia Melaka. Spanning 119 pages, it is organized into five major parts (Bahagian A–E) and eight normative appendices (Lampiran A–H):

  • Bahagian A – Permulaan (Bab 1–5, pp. 8–16): Background, objectives, Malaysian Qualifications Framework (MQF) alignment, scope, definitions, glossary, and acronyms.
  • Bahagian B – Konsep, Prinsip dan Kerangka (Bab 6–8, pp. 17–30): Three micro-credential categories (UMC, SAMC, MCE), constructive alignment, Student Learning Time (SLT), assessment, and the 3-tier recognition hierarchy (Rajah 1–7, Jadual 1).
  • Bahagian C – Pembangunan, Pelaksanaan dan Jaminan Kualiti (Bab 9–11, pp. 31–43): Development lifecycle, academic review, Senate approval, cohort delivery, readiness checklists, and Quality Verification (QV) for SAMC (Rajah 8–13).
  • Bahagian D – Pengumpulan, Pemindahan Kredit dan Stackable Degree (Bab 12–14, pp. 44–58): Academic Credit Record (ACR), Academic Bank of Credit (ABC) deposits, single redemption principle, 80% LO-matching credit transfer, and stackable pathways with institutional residency (Rajah 14–18).
  • Bahagian E – Tadbir Urus, Pemantauan dan Pengurusan Rekod (Bab 15–18, pp. 59–71): University, Faculty, and Operational governance tiers, RACI responsibilities, CQI cycles, version management, mismatch reconciliation, retention, and audit trails (Rajah 19, Jadual 2).
  • Lampiran A–H (pp. 75–119): Standardized templates, readiness checklists, CQI forms, QV evidence dossiers, digital badge/credential specifications, credit transfer evaluation matrices, stackable pathway mappings, and governance matrices.

3. Cross-Role End-to-End Workflow Verification

3.1 Workflow Architecture Overview

A core requirement of this audit is verifying complete, end-to-end workflows across all identified user personas: Student, Lecturer / Proposer, Academic Reviewer, Deputy Dean (Academic), Senate / Central University, CODL Administrator, and Industry Expert. The system must not merely present isolated screens, but enforce strict procedural handoffs, automated state transitions, audit logging, and role-based data isolation.

3.2 Verification of 11 Core Functional Workflows

Workflow 1: Proposal Development, Multi-Tier Review & Senate Approval

  • Initiator: Lecturer / Proposer initiates proposal via /curriculum/wizard (answering the 3 category questions from Rajah 3).
  • Authoring: Proposer completes 9 tabs in /curriculum/proposals/{id} (Basics, Modules, Justification, Outcomes, Assessment, Credential, Readiness, Comments, Submit) matching Lampiran A. Live validation in ProposalChecker.php highlights blocking errors and warnings.
  • Reviewer Assignment: Deputy Dean assigns 1 or more academic reviewers and optional industry experts (/approval/proposals/{id}/reviewers), setting due dates and scope.
  • Conflict of Interest: Assigned reviewers must complete a mandatory COI declaration before the proposal dossier unlocks (reviews/coi.blade.php). If conflict declared, reviewer is unassigned and Deputy Dean notified.
  • Evaluation Form: Reviewers rate 8 standard criteria (plus parent-course mapping for UMC), log tracked comments, and submit recommendation (Endorse, Amend, Not Supported). Industry experts provide advisory comments only without voting power (Bab 16.7).
  • Resolution Gate: Proposer answers comments. Deputy Dean verifies resolution. Endorsement is blocked while any blocking comment remains open.
  • Endorsement & Senate Decision: Deputy Dean endorses proposal, verifying the category. Central authority records Senate decision with official meeting reference and minute date (/approval/proposals/{id}/senate), generating the official Senate Decision Pack (/approval/proposals/{id}/pack).
  • Downstream Impact: Approved proposal generates Version 1.0 (mc_versions), locks content, and enables cohort creation in Offering module.
  • Status & Evidence: PASS • Verified in CurriculumTest.php and ApprovalTest.php.

Workflow 2: Cohort Setup, Offering Readiness Checklist & Publishing

  • Initiator: Lecturer creates a cohort offering for an approved version (/offerings/create), defining dates, fees, intake capacity, and delivery mode.
  • Readiness Checklist (Lampiran B1–B7): Lecturer completes items covering trainer readiness, materials upload, platform availability (uLearn/UCreds), and assessment rubrics.
  • Deputy Dean Confirmation: Deputy Dean inspects evidence and independently confirms readiness (/offerings/{id}/readiness). Status transitions from Not Ready to Ready, and automatically opens for enrolment when start date arrives.
  • Public Publication: Offering appears on the public catalogue (/catalogue) with transparent information, MQF/credit claims derived strictly from verified records, and automated scanning for forbidden promotional claims (ForbiddenClaims.php).
  • Status & Evidence: PASS • Verified in OfferingTest.php.

Workflow 3: Participant Registration, PDPA Consent & Payment

  • Initiator: Prospective participant visits /offerings/{id} and initiates registration without requiring prior university sign-in.
  • Step 1 (Eligibility & Profile): User verifies prerequisite criteria, enters IC/passport and contact details.
  • Step 2 (Recognition & PDPA Notice): Mandatory acknowledgement of recognition wording (confirming UMC does not guarantee degree admission) and consent to personal data processing under PDPA.
  • Step 3 (Payment): Payment processed via idempotent adapter (StubPaymentAdapter.php). Card numbers are never stored; transaction reference logged in payments table.
  • Step 4 (Confirmation & Instructions): System generates enrolment record, assigns Enrolment ID, and renders official confirmation letter with uLearn/UCreds access instructions (/student/enrolment/{id}/letter).
  • Status & Evidence: PASS • Verified in EnrolmentTest.php.

Workflow 4: Learning Exchange, Assessment Marks & Result Verification

  • Learning Exchange: CODL Administrator exports participant roster manifest (CSV) and imports assessment marks/badge completion records from UCreds/Moodle via ImportValidator.php with pre-validation report.
  • Mark Entry: Lecturer accesses cohort gradebook (/results/{cohortId}), inputs assessment marks against approved assessment plan, and submits marks.
  • Integrity Holds: If an academic integrity case is flagged, the participant's results are placed in Held status, blocking achievement finalization.
  • Verification: Deputy Dean reviews grade distribution and verifies results by cohort or module (/results/{cohortId}/verify). Proposer cannot verify own marks (separation of duties). Results become visible to students only after verification.
  • Result Revision: Any post-verification result change requires formal change request, justification, and Deputy Dean sign-off, preserving full audit history.
  • Status & Evidence: PASS • Verified in ResultsTest.php.

Workflow 5: Completion Confirmation, Open Badges 3.0 & Credential Issuance

  • Completion Evaluation: System evaluates whether participant has achieved all modules, met whole-MC assessments, and resolved integrity holds (Completion.php).
  • Deputy Dean Confirmation: Deputy Dean reviews pre-issue checklist and confirms completion (/completion/{cohortId}). Dispatches CompletionConfirmed event.
  • Automated Credentialing: Event listener (IssueCredential.php) automatically mints Digital Badges for each module and issues the official bilingual A4 Certificate (DOC-03) and signed W3C VC-JWT Digital Credential (DOC-04) using Ed25519 signing keys.
  • Public Verification: Anyone can verify credential validity via GET /verify/{code} without login, inspecting cryptographic proofs and JWKS public keys.
  • Correction / Revocation: Deputy Dean can correct clerical errors (original kept), replace credentials, or revoke fraudulent credentials with public status display.
  • Status & Evidence: PASS • Verified in CompletionTest.php and CredentialTest.php.

Workflow 6: Stand-Alone Micro-Credential Quality Verification (QV) Dossier

  • Eligibility Check: Lecturer initiates QV application (/quality/{mcId}). System validates: SAMC category, ≥40 hours SLT, and at least 1 completed cohort with verified results.
  • Evidence Compilation: System auto-links internal data for 6 evidence groups matching Lampiran D (academic design, assessment rubrics, cohort performance, CQI records, digital credential metadata).
  • Dual Endorsement: Lecturer marks dossier as ready; Deputy Dean verifies and confirms internal readiness.
  • Submission & External Decision: Deputy Dean records external QVC/MQA submission reference and eventual verification decision (credit value, MQF level, validity dates up to 3 years). Verified record acts as single source of truth for catalogue claims.
  • Status & Evidence: PASS • Verified in QualityTest.php.

Workflow 7: Credit Accumulation, Consent, ACR Generation & ABC Deposit

  • ACR Preparation: Upon completion of an eligible UMC or Quality-Verified SAMC, system prepares an Academic Credit Record (ACR) following 7 automated checks (acr_checks). MCE is blocked immediately.
  • Participant Consent: Participant receives notification and submits consent choice (Consented, Declined, Postponed) via /student/acr/{id}/consent.
  • Deputy Dean Review: Deputy Dean confirms ACR record accuracy.
  • ABC Export & Deposit: Consented ACRs enter ABC deposit queue (/creditbank/queue), exported as CSV batch, and deposit logged with ABC central reference. Credits become Available in student's credit ledger (/creditbank/mine) with a 10-year expiration timer.
  • Status & Evidence: PASS (with CSV batch export; live national API gap noted) • Verified in CreditBankTest.php.

Workflow 8: Credit Transfer Evaluation, 80% LO Match & Single Redemption

  • Application: Student applies for credit transfer to an enrolled degree programme using Available ABC credits (/transfer/apply). MCE and unverified SAMC blocked.
  • Validity & Eligibility: Receiving Faculty Deputy Dean evaluates application against 9 criteria in Lampiran F7.
  • LO Equivalence Matrix: Evaluator maps applicant's MC learning outcomes against target course learning outcomes side-by-side. System computes percentage match; ≥80% threshold strictly enforced before approval can proceed.
  • Single Redemption Lock: On approval, credit ledger immediately places credits in Provisionally Redeemed status, blocking reuse in other programs. Upon degree graduation, credit transitions to Redeemed. If student withdraws, credit reverts to Available.
  • Decision Letter: Official printable transfer decision letter (DOC-12) generated with detailed course exemptions and conditions.
  • Status & Evidence: PASS • Verified in TransferTest.php.

Workflow 9: Stackable Degree Pathway Mapping & Individual Study Planning

  • Pathway Definition: Faculty defines official stackable pathway (/pathways/create) mapping multiple credit-bearing MCs to degree program courses. Senate approval reference recorded before publishing.
  • Curriculum Coverage: Maps target courses to Programme Learning Outcomes (PLO) and Body of Knowledge (BoK) domains (Lampiran G4 & G5).
  • Individual Study Plan (Lampiran G8): Student or advisor builds individual study plan (/pathways/{id}/plan). System verifies:
    1. Home Credit Rule (Clause 14.5.1): Computes % of credits earned directly from UTeM (≥30% mandatory threshold).
    2. Conventional Residency Rule (Clause 14.5.3): Enforces minimum 1 semester physical residency at UTeM for conventional programmes.
    3. Remaining Requirements: Compiles remaining core, elective, FYP, and industrial training courses needed for graduation.
  • Suitability Verdict: Produces structured decision: Suitable to Proceed, Suitable with Conditions, Not Yet Suitable, or Not Suitable.
  • Status & Evidence: PASS • Verified in PathwayTest.php.

Workflow 10: Continuous Quality Improvement (CQI) & Version Management

  • Cohort Metrics: Monitoring dashboard tracks active rate, completion rate, pass rate, mark distribution, and CLO attainment against institutional targets.
  • Student Feedback: Anonymous survey submitted by participants after completion (/student/feedback/{id}).
  • CQI Record (Lampiran C): Lecturer evaluates 6 quality domains, records identified issues with root cause, impact, and priority, and proposes action plans with owners and success KPIs.
  • Effectiveness Loop: Actions move step-by-step; lecturer evaluates effectiveness, and only Deputy Dean can formally close the action. Ineffective actions carry forward to next cycle.
  • Version Management: Changes classified automatically as Minor (recorded for CQI) or Material (triggers new draft version, full Senate re-approval, and transition rule execution).
  • Status & Evidence: PASS • Verified in MonitoringTest.php and VersionTest.php.

Workflow 11: Governance, Audit Integrity, Discrepancies & Retention

  • Separation of Duties: Verified across all routes. Academic decisions locked to Faculty/Senate. CODL restricted to operations, templates, reference data, and reporting.
  • Audit Trail: Cryptographic SHA-256 hash chaining on all audit_events. Database trigger blocks updates or deletions. Verification job detects chain breaks.
  • Mismatch Log: Background scanner cross-checks results, credentials, ACRs, and ABC deposits. Found discrepancies can only be resolved via audited correction records.
  • Retention & Disposal: Non-academic logs undergo retention countdowns and require formal approval for disposal. Academic student records protected by permanent retention rules (in perpetuity).
  • Status & Evidence: PASS • Verified in RecordsTest.php and RbacSecurityTest.php.
