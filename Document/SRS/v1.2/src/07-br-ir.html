4. Business Rules

This section lists the rules of the Guide that MCMS enforces, as a separate requirement category (D-25). A business rule is binding on the complete system under UTeM policy, whatever the release (D-24). The column Enforced by names the functional requirements that put the rule into effect; acceptance tests SHALL cover every rule. Rules BR-01 to BR-06 keep the numbers given in the Glossary and Decision Log; their values are settings ({{tab:settings}}).

5. External Interface Requirements

5.1 User Interfaces

MCMS has three web front ends sharing one design: the staff portal (lecturers, deputy deans, CODL administrators and invited experts), the participant portal (P8) and the public pages (catalogue and credential verification). {{fig:screenmap}} shows the main screens by audience. Detailed layouts belong to the SDD and the design work.

5.2 Hardware Interfaces

MCMS has no special hardware interface. It runs on standard UTeM server infrastructure.

5.3 Software Interfaces

All external systems are reached through adapters. Each adapter has a fixed interface towards the MCMS modules and one or more implementations: stub (simulated, for development and testing), manual (staff exchange files or enter data), and live (automated connection).
