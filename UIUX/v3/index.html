Design System Tokens & Rules

Design Tokens & Style Enforcements

1. Core Light Color Palette

--color-primary: #0052ff
--color-primary-deep: #0a192f
--color-sidebar-bg: #0b1a3d
--color-ink: #090a0f (18:1)
--color-ink-muted: #475569 (4.6:1)
--color-line-hairline: #e2e8f0

2. Controlled Micro-Radius

--radius-xs: 2px (Micro tags, inline badges)
--radius-sm: 4px (Buttons, precision inputs)
--radius-md: 6px (Standard cards, bento cells)
--radius-lg: 8px (Hero containers, modals)
--radius-search-pill: 12px (Search input)
--radius-sidebar-corner: 28px (Brand header contour)
--radius-pill: 9999px (Status indicator pips only)

3. Typographic Markers (D-53)

No Generic Icons: Zero icon fonts, emojis, or stock SVGs.
Section Tags: [F1] through [F11]
Role Tags: [P1] through [P8]
Status Pills: Must have text prefix ([STATUS: VALID])
Toggles: [+], [-], <, >
