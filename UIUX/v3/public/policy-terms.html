Terms of Use

Terms and conditions governing the use of the UTeM Micro-Credential Management System portal.

1. Authorized Use

This portal is provided by Universiti Teknikal Malaysia Melaka for accredited educational delivery, credential verification, and academic credit administration. Unauthorized tampering with cryptographic artifacts or automated scraping is prohibited.

2. Credential Authenticity

A digital credential or badge is authentic only when verified directly against UTeM's official verification endpoint or cryptographically confirmed against UTeM's published Ed25519 signing keys.

3. Credit Recognition & Transfer

Completing an offering does not guarantee an Academic Credit Record (ACR), an ABC deposit, or credit exemption into any university degree. Each administrative step is governed by separate regulatory evaluation.

4. Fee & Refund Policy

Tuition fees and refund requests prior to course commencement are handled case-by-case in accordance with UTeM Financial By-laws and CODL administrative procedures.
