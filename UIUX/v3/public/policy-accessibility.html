Accessibility Statement

Our commitment to ensuring digital accessibility for people of all abilities.

1. Our Accessibility Goal

We target full conformance with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA. Every page is engineered to work seamlessly with keyboard navigation, screen readers, and high-contrast display modes without loss of functionality.

2. Color Contrast Standards

All body copy meets or exceeds the mandatory 4.5:1 contrast ratio against the white canvas (#090a0f ink provides ~18:1; #475569 ink-muted provides ~4.6:1). Decorative lines (#94a3b8) are strictly prohibited for readable text. Status indicators never rely on color alone.

3. Assistive Feedback & Support

If you experience any barriers accessing information on this portal, please contact CODL at codl@utem.edu.my. We will provide alternative accessible formats within two working days.
