Frequently Asked Questions

Official answers regarding credentials, academic credit transfer, registration, and data privacy.

What is the difference between a digital badge and a credential certificate?

A digital badge is awarded for each modular component you successfully complete and pass within an offering. The credential certificate is issued when you complete all required modules and results are formally ratified by the faculty.

Does completing a micro-credential automatically give me academic credit?

Only Unbundled Micro-Credentials (UMC) and Standalone Micro-Credentials (SAMC) with active Quality Verification (QV) lead to academic credit. Micro-Credential Eksekutif (MCE) provides a Certificate of Competency without credit.

What is Quality Verification (QV) and why does it matter?

Quality Verification confirms that a standalone micro-credential adheres to national academic standards. If QV has expired for a cohort, credit transfer is paused until the faculty completes its renewal audit.

What is an Academic Credit Record (ACR)?

An Academic Credit Record (ACR) is an official institutional document issued by UTeM certifying the credit value earned from an accredited micro-credential. It is an official record, not an automated transfer.

What is the Academic Bank of Credit (ABC) and is deposit mandatory?

ABC is a national repository storing higher-education credits for 10 years. Depositing credit requires your explicit consent; you are free to consent, decline, or postpone deposit at any time.

Can I use my earned micro-credential credit in an undergraduate degree?

Yes, upon formal admission into an accredited programme. The faculty evaluates syllabus overlap (minimum 80%), stackable credit is capped at 30% of total degree credits, and credits can be redeemed only once.

Will stacking micro-credentials automatically award me a degree?

No. Micro-credentials provide modular exemption. You must fulfill all degree curriculum requirements, including institutional residency, capstone final projects, and university core subjects.

How much do offerings cost and what is the refund policy?

The tuition fee is clearly listed on each offering detail page. Fee refunds prior to cohort commencement are evaluated case-by-case by the Centre for Open & Distance Learning (CODL).

I am not an existing UTeM student. Can I register?

Yes. External working adults and lifelong learners can register through the CODL portal. A temporary learner ID is issued to access course learning materials.

Which language is used for delivery?

The delivery language (English or Bahasa Melayu) is specified on each offering's syllabus page. Course assessments and official credential transcripts are fully bilingual.

How do employers or verifiers check a credential?

Anyone can navigate to "Verify a Credential" and enter the 10-character code printed on the certificate, or upload the signed JSON-LD / JWT file. Public verification displays name, credential, issuer, date, and status only.

What happens if a credential is revoked or superseded?

A revoked credential shows clearly as revoked with an official justification. A superseded credential indicates that an updated or corrected version exists and provides a link to the new certificate.

Who has access to my personal academic records?

Public verification discloses only your name and completion status; it never discloses numerical exam grades, marks, or personal identification numbers, strictly adhering to the Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA 2010).
