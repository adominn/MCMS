2. STATUTORY REGULATIONS & EXEMPTION CONDITIONS

  1. All micro-credentials submitted for credit transfer must carry a valid Academic Credit Record (ACR) and be confirmed in the Academic Bank of Credit (ABC).
  2. Credit transfer requires an earned minimum grade of 'C' (2.00 GP) or higher in each component micro-credential.
  3. Per Malaysian Qualifications Agency (MQA) regulations, credit transferred through micro-credentials cannot exceed 30% of total graduation credits.
  4. Once redeemed against a degree programme, credits cannot be reused for another credential.
