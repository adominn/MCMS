Privacy Notice

Summary of how Universiti Teknikal Malaysia Melaka handles personal data in the Micro-Credential Management System (Personal Data Protection Act 2010).

1. What We Collect

Your full legal name, national identity number (MyKad / Passport hash), contact email, learner ID, enrollment logs, assessment scores, and the digital credentials and badges issued to you.

2. Why We Collect It

To administer course enrollments, verify continuous assessments and exam outcomes, mint cryptographically verifiable digital credentials, and — strictly with your consent — transmit and deposit credits in the national Academic Bank of Credit (ABC).

3. Who Sees Your Information

University academic staff and administrators access only the data required for their specific role. Public credential verification displays only your name, credential title, issuer, issue date, and validity status. Numerical scores and GPA are never displayed publicly.

4. Your Choices & Rights

You may request to inspect or correct your personal data at any time. You may also decline or postpone an Academic Bank of Credit (ABC) deposit without penalty. Contact CODL at codl@utem.edu.my.
