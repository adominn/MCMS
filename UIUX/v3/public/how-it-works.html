The 8-Stage Credit Lifecycle

At Universiti Teknikal Malaysia Melaka, earning a micro-credential and transferring academic credit are distinct, independently evaluated milestones. Completing a course does not automatically grant credit transfer or university admission.

Regulatory Governance Flow (Cl. 10.3.1 & Appendix H): Progression from Course Achievement (Stages 01–02) to ACR Issuance (Stage 03) establishes an official, signed academic credential. ABC deposit (Stages 04–05) provides secure archival storage only. Credit transfer (Stages 06–08) is an independent evaluation conducted by the receiving faculty requiring 80% syllabus equivalence and Senate endorsement.
STAGE 01 & 02 P1 Lecturer & P2 Deputy Dean

1. Module Badge & Course Credential

What it is: Digital badges are awarded for each passing modular component. A signed, verifiable certificate is minted when the complete micro-credential is concluded.
What it is not: It is not a formal university degree qualification and carries no academic credit by itself without an ACR.
STAGE 03 P6 Records Custodian

2. Academic Credit Record (ACR)

What it is: UTeM formally registers the credit hours achieved. ACR is strictly granted to Unbundled Micro-Credentials (UMC) and Standalone Micro-Credentials (SAMC) with active Quality Verification.
What it is not: It is not a credit transfer. MCE executive courses never receive an ACR. Storing is not transferring.
STAGE 04 & 05 P7 ABC Depositor

3. Academic Bank of Credit (ABC) Deposit

What it is: Only upon explicit learner consent, your verified credit record is transmitted to the national Academic Bank of Credit (ABC) where it remains valid for 10 years.
What it is not: Storing is not transferring. ABC is a repository storage vault only. Depositing does not grant automatic admission.
STAGE 06, 07 & 08 P3 Senate & Faculty

4. Academic Credit Transfer & Redemption

What it is: When enrolled in a full degree program, you submit your ACR for exemption. The faculty committee evaluates 80% syllabus overlap course-by-course and Senate renders final approval.
What it is not: Transfer is not guaranteed. Stacked credits are capped at 30% of total degree credits, and each credit can be redeemed only once.
UTeM Guide Compliance Notice – Cl. 10.3.1 Completion, credential minting, ACR issuance, ABC storage, and credit transfer are distinct administrative stages. Completing a micro-credential does not guarantee an ACR, deposit in ABC, or that any receiving institution will grant credit exemption. Learners may pause or stop at any milestone.
