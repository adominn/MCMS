What this does and does not give you
This is an Unbundled Micro-Credential (UMC) from BENE 1123 Electric Circuit Theory. Completing the whole UMC carries the credit of the parent course. A module or a badge on its own is not completion and not credit.
Credit: 3
Completing this micro-credential, receiving an Academic Credit Record, depositing credit to the Academic Bank of Credit and transferring credit are separate decisions.
Learning outcomes
Analyze complex multi-loop circuits using network theorems.
