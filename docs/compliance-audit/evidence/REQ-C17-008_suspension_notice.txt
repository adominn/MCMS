Cloud Native Development: Suspension notice
Reference: NOT-2-2026 · Effective: 20 Oct 2026 · Fakulti Teknologi Maklumat Dan Komunikasi
To all participants registered in Cloud Native Development.
The Fakulti Teknologi Maklumat Dan Komunikasi has decided on the suspension of Cloud Native Development, effective 20 Oct 2026. Reason: Lab cloud sandbox security patch and tenant isolation update.. This notice explains what it means for you.
Who is affected
Cohort	Dates	Status	Participants
2027/1	04 Dec 2026 to 04 Mar 2027	Open	1
2026/S1	25 Sep 2026 to 04 Dec 2026	Running	1
Total participants	2
Transition plan
Enrolled students will have access extended by 21 days upon resumption.
Credentials and credit you have already earned stay valid.
Certificates, Digital Credentials, Academic Credit Records and any ABC deposit are not changed by this decision.
Questions: codl@utem.edu.my, 06-270 2000 (08:00 to 17:00, Monday to Friday).
Farid Hashim
