Academic Credit Policy
Policy

An Academic Credit Record (ACR) is a separate record from your certificate and Digital Credential. It is retained permanently and cannot be edited directly; any correction is logged in the official correction audit log.

10-Year ABC Validity
Credits deposited in ABC remain valid for 10 years from the deposit date.
Single Redemption Principle
Once redeemed toward a qualification, credit cannot be reused for another qualification.
Storage Only
ABC deposit is storage only and never grants automatic credit transfer or programme admission.
