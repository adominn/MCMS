Senate-approved official degree articulation roadmaps for credit transfer into UTeM degree programmes
Academic Regulatory Notice
An approved pathway transfers no credit: each credit goes through credit transfer evaluation by the receiving programme upon formal admission. No micro-credential leads to a degree automatically, however many are collected. At least 30% of the graduating credit must be earned directly at UTeM under residency regulations.

Academic Articulation Principles & Regulations
At least 30% of degree credits at UTeM

At least 30% of graduating credits must be completed directly as a registered UTeM student; transferred credits cannot exceed this threshold.

Campus Residency Requirement

Conventional degree programmes require at least one registered semester in physical campus residency before degree conferment.

Admission & Matriculation Separate

Credit transfer requires an admitted student. Admission is an independent evaluation conducted by the Admissions Office (Pejabat Pendaftar).

10-Year Credit Expiry

Earned micro-credential credits are valid for transfer consideration up to 10 years from their deposit date in the Academic Bank of Credit (ABC).
