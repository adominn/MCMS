BITS - Bachelor of Computer Science (Software Development) with Honours
MQF Level 6 · 120 graduating credits · Conventional · Senate Reference: Senate 7/2026 (04/08/2026)
Official Articulation Roadmap:
This document lists the credit-bearing micro-credentials approved by the Senate for articulation into this undergraduate degree programme. A pathway transfers no credit by itself; each participant's credit is decided individually through credit transfer upon official Pejabat Pendaftar admission. At least 30% of graduating credits must be earned directly at UTeM under residency rules.

Academic Governance & Regulatory Notice: No micro-credential leads to a degree automatically, however many are accumulated. Credit exemption is subject to faculty approval, valid Academic Bank of Credit (ABC) deposits within 10 years of completion, and compliance with the 30% UTeM minimum home-credit requirement.
