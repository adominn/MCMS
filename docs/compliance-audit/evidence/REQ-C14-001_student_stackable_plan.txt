My degree plan
Your plan is faculty advice on how your micro-credentials could count toward a degree programme.
A pathway is not admission, a credit transfer or an award. No number of micro-credentials or badges leads to a degree automatically. Credit goes through credit transfer evaluation by the receiving programme, credit is valid for a limited time, and you must meet the graduation conditions of the programme.
